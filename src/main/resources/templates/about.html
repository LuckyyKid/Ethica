Our Story

Why We Built Ethica

01

The Problem We Saw

Most young investors feel lost when it comes to investing. Overwhelmed by thousands of stock options, complex financial jargon, and the fear of making mistakes, many either avoid investing altogether or fall victim to scams promising quick returns.

02

The Buffett Principle

Warren Buffett's timeless wisdom inspired us: invest only in what you understand. He calls it staying within your "circle of competence." The size of this circle doesn't matter what matters is knowing its boundaries. This simple principle has guided the most successful investors for decades.

03

Values Meet Returns

We noticed a growing demand: people want to invest in causes they care about. Whether it's sustainability, technology, healthcare, or social impact investors want their money to reflect their values. We asked: why not build wealth AND support what matters to you?

04

The Ethica Solution

Ethica helps you discover investments that match your interests, values, and understanding. By giving you a reason beyond just financial returns, we help you stay committed, avoid scams, and build a portfolio you truly believe in. No more overthinking which stocks to choose invest in what resonates with you.
